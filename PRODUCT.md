# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: students, educators, and aspiring innovators from roughly 20 schools and colleges in Nepal, deciding whether to attend the STEAM, Astronomy and Aerospace Camp or the free AI and Space Conference. Secondary: technology enthusiasts, exhibitors, and the general public browsing the Startup Exhibition and Technology Exhibition. Both arrive wanting the practical facts fast: what the event is, when and where it happens, and what they can do or see there.

## Product Purpose
A promotional, informational multi-page site for Startup and Idea Fest 2026, a one-day event in Nepal combining a Startup Exhibition, an AI and Space Conference, and a hands-on STEAM/Astronomy/Aerospace Camp. It exists to communicate what the event covers, who it's for, and the practical details (date, venue, organizers), and to build excitement ahead of the event. Success is a visitor understanding the event's scope and leaving knowing where and when to show up.

## Positioning
Where ideas meet technology: a single day where students, startups, and space-tech converge in one venue, in Nepal.

## Brand Personality
Ambitious and hands-on rather than corporate: the event is built around building, flying, and demonstrating real hardware (rockets, drones, satellites, robotics), so the site should feel technical and youthful, not glossy-SaaS. Inferred from the brief's own emphasis on "learning by doing" and student-built projects; not yet confirmed with the organizers.

## Anti-references
Not a generic SaaS landing page (no hero-metric gradient cards, no stock-photo "team collaborating in office" imagery). Not a dry institutional notice either; this is a youth-facing event, not a government circular.

## Design Principles
- Ground the visual language in the actual subject (orbits, flight paths, space/night-sky) rather than generic tech decoration.
- Keep the palette bluish, anchored in the two real organizer marks (TSCM Aerospace's navy, the Startup & Idea Fest logo's teal), not an invented brand color.
- Lead with real event facts (date, venue, numbers) over marketing abstraction; this is a notice people act on, not a brand story.
- One moment of boldness (the hero), quiet and disciplined everywhere else.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No stated requirements from the brief. Build to WCAG AA by default (contrast, focus states, reduced motion).
